New Publication: 16S-Pipeline in Journal of Microbiology

Published on May 14, 2026

We are pleased to announce that our protocol paper, "16S-Pipeline: A comprehensive web-based platform for end-to-end 16S rRNA amplicon sequencing analysis," has been published in the Journal of Microbiology (IF 2.6; JCR 60.4%).

16S-Pipeline is an open-source, web-based platform that delivers a complete workflow from raw FASTQ files to publication-ready statistical analyses. The pipeline automatically detects sequencing type (paired-end, single-end, long-read), variable region, and sequencing platform (Illumina, PacBio HiFi, Nanopore), then performs:

  • Quality filtering and primer trimming
  • ASV inference via DADA2
  • Taxonomy assignment against SILVA v138.1
  • Phylogenetic tree construction
  • Optional functional prediction via PICRUSt2

Downstream analyses include alpha and beta diversity, taxonomic composition visualization, and differential abundance testing using five complementary methods (ALDEx2, DESeq2, ANCOM-BC2, LinDA, MaAsLin2) with consensus reporting, as well as KEGG pathway mapping. Built-in NCBI SRA integration enables downloading public datasets for re-analysis and generates submission metadata spreadsheets for data deposition.

The interactive web interface is built on FastAPI and Plotly Dash, enabling researchers to perform complex microbiome analyses without command-line expertise.

16S-Pipeline is freely available at github.com/tatsu1207/16S-Pipeline under the MIT License.
