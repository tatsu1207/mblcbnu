New Publication by Geon Choi: 16S rRNA Gene Copy Number Variation in Microbial Source Tracking

Published on April 28, 2026

Congratulations to Geon Choi on his latest first-author publication in Applied Biological Chemistry (IF 2.7; JCR 53%):

"16S rRNA gene copy number variation and sink biomass shape source contributions in community-wide microbial source tracking"

Geon Choi and Tatsuya Unno*

This study examines how two often-overlooked factors — variation in 16S rRNA gene copy numbers across taxa and the biomass of the sink environment — can systematically bias source contribution estimates in community-wide microbial source tracking (MST). The work offers practical guidance for interpreting MST results in environmental and public health contexts.

This paper builds on Geon's growing body of work in microbial source tracking and continues our lab's effort to refine bioinformatic tools for environmental microbiome analysis.
