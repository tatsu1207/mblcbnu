New Publication by Geon Choi: Microbial Dynamics in Livestock Manure and Wastewater Treatment

Published on October 17, 2025

Congratulations to Geon Choi on his first-author publication in Applied Biological Chemistry (IF 2.7; JCR 53%):

"Quantitative assessment of microbial dynamics in livestock manure and municipal wastewater treatment plants"

Geon Choi, Hokyung Song and Tatsuya Unno*

In this study, Geon and colleagues quantitatively profile how microbial communities change throughout livestock manure handling and municipal wastewater treatment processes. The findings provide a baseline for understanding microbial flow from agricultural and urban sources into receiving environments — directly relevant to monitoring antibiotic-resistance gene dissemination and assessing treatment efficacy.

This work lays important groundwork for the source-tracking research that continues in our lab.
