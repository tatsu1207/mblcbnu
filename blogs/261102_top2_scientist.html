Dr. Tatsuya Unno Listed Among the World's Top 2% Scientists Two Years in a Row

Published on Nov 02, 2026

We are pleased to announce that Dr. Tatsuya Unno, Principal Investigator of the Microbiome Lab, ranks within the top 2% of scientists in Microbiology for the second year in a row in the science-wide citation database compiled by John P.A. Ioannidis and colleagues at Stanford University with Elsevier, widely known as the "World's Top 2% Scientists" list.

The database uses Scopus data and ranks researchers by a composite citation score (c-score). Excluding self-citations, Dr. Unno's single-year rankings in the Microbiology subfield are:

  • 2025 edition (citations received in 2024): rank 4,192 of 217,955 (top 1.92%), c-score 2.46
  • 2026 edition (citations received in 2025): rank 4,205 of 232,186 (top 1.81%), c-score 2.47

This recognition reflects the work of the lab's students, researchers, and collaborators in microbiome science, antimicrobial resistance, and bioinformatics. Congratulations, Dr. Unno, and thank you to everyone who contributed to this research!

Both editions are publicly available. To check these figures, open the single-year "Table_1_Authors_singleyr" file in each dataset and search for "Unno, Tatsuya".
