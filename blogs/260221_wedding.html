Wedding of Sunwoo Lee & Youjin Jeong

February 21, 2026

We are delighted to share that two of our former lab members, Sunwoo Lee and Youjin Jeong, were married on Saturday, February 21, 2026 in Daejeon. It was a beautiful ceremony celebrating the union of two wonderful people who first met during their time in the Microbiome Lab.

Prof. Tatsuya Unno had the honor of delivering the 주례사 (officiant's address) at the ceremony, offering words of wisdom and warm wishes to the newlyweds on behalf of the entire lab family.

Congratulations to Sunwoo and Youjin! We wish them a lifetime of happiness together.
